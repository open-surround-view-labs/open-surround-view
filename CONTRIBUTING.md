# Contributing

Contributions that improve Android head-unit compatibility, camera discovery, documentation, tests, or privacy are welcome.

1. Create a focused branch.
2. Keep device-specific behavior isolated and documented.
3. Test the affected Camera2 or MJPEG path when hardware is available.
4. Describe the Android version and generic hardware characteristics without sharing serial numbers, private addresses, credentials, identifiable images, or other personal data.
5. Open a pull request describing the change and its validation.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the MIT License.
